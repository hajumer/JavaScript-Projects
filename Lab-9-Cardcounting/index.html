Card Counting Assistant

About This Project

This project is a simple Card Counting Assistant built with JavaScript. It keeps track of cards and helps decide whether the player should Bet or Hold.

In this project, I practiced functions, global variables, if/else statements, increment and decrement operators, and conditional logic.

Card Counting Rules

Cards 2 to 6 increase the count by 1. Cards 7 to 9 do not change the count. Cards 10, J, Q, K, and A decrease the count by 1.

If the count is positive, the result is Bet. If the count is zero or negative, the result is Hold.
