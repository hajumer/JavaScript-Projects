Leap Year Calculator

About This Project

This project checks whether a given year is a leap year using JavaScript.

In this project, I practiced functions, if/else statements, the modulus operator, logical operators, variables, and return statements.

Leap Year Rules

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except years divisible by 100 unless they are also divisible by 400.

Examples

2024 → Leap Year

2000 → Leap Year

1900 → Not a Leap Year
